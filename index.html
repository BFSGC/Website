The Format

What are ChatGPT Ads?

Sponsored listings that appear below ChatGPT's answers — clearly labelled and separate from the answer itself. Businesses pay per view or per click to reach people while they compare options in a conversation.

Live in Singapore

Now in 60+ countries — Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam and Taiwan included.

Who sees ads

Free and Go plans only. Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise are ad-free; no ads to under-18 accounts.

Where ads appear

Below the answer, labelled Sponsored. Ads never change what ChatGPT recommends.

What an ad contains

Advertiser name, logo, headline, description, an image and a link to your landing page.

Anatomy of a ChatGPT ad — and how it's chosen

Ad anatomy: advertiser name → logo → "Sponsored" label → headline → description → image & landing page. OpenAI matches ads by conversation context, your landing page, copy and the context hints you give each ad group — hints guide matching, they don't guarantee a placement.

What you never get: conversations stay private from advertisers, and ads never appear next to sensitive chats (e.g. health).

Pricing

How It's Paid

Three ways to pay, one simple rule. You set a daily budget and a maximum bid; OpenAI's auction is relevance-weighted and second-price, so you often pay less than your maximum.

Reach · CPM

Pay per 1,000 views. For awareness and reach when buyers are early in their decision.

Clicks · CPC

Pay per click to drive traffic. OpenAI suggests starting at a US$3–5 maximum CPC.

Conversions · oCPC

Conversion-optimised, billed per valid click. Requires conversion measurement set-up.

The budget rule that surprises everyone

A daily budget is an average over seven days: one day can spend up to 2× the budget, but a week never more than 7× it. The minimum varies by market and currency — we'll show you the Singapore-dollar figure in your account.

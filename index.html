The format

What ChatGPT Ads are

Sponsored placements that appear below ChatGPT's answers — clearly labelled, kept separate from the answer, and shown only to users on Free and Go plans.

Reach people mid-decision

Your ad appears while someone is comparing options in a real conversation — with context that matches your product.

Pay per view, click or conversion

CPM for reach, CPC for traffic, or conversion-optimised campaigns billed per valid click.

Private by design

OpenAI never shares conversations with advertisers. Ads are kept away from sensitive chats.

FAQ

Quick answers

Who sees ChatGPT Ads?
Users on the Free and Go plans. Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise are ad-free, and ads are not shown to accounts identified as under 18.
Do ads change ChatGPT's answers?
No. Ads run on separate systems from the chat model. Advertisers have no ability to shape, rank or alter answers.
Is my data shared with advertisers?
No. OpenAI never shares conversations with advertisers and never sells user data. Advertisers see only aggregated, non-identifying performance.
Where do ads appear?
Below ChatGPT's answers, clearly labelled sponsored. Not in the Atlas browser, not in Temporary Chats, and not near sensitive topics like personal health or politics.
What does the diagnosis cost?
Nothing. The readiness diagnosis is free — you get an eligibility check, a campaign outline and a benchmark snapshot, with a clear SLA for the report.
